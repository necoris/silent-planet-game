# Silent Planet

**Silent Planet** is a sci-fi exploration and investigation RPG developed in Unreal Engine 5 using C++ and Blueprints.

The game follows a space mission that takes an unexpected turn, leaving the protagonist alone aboard a mysterious spacecraft. Through exploration, investigation, environmental storytelling, and interaction with the ship's AI, the player must uncover the truth behind the incident and discover what happened to the missing crew.

---

## About the Game

Silent Planet is a single-player sci-fi adventure focused on exploration, narrative investigation, and environmental puzzles.

The player explores the spacecraft, investigates its abandoned sections, collects evidence, reads crew logs, interacts with terminals, and gradually reconstructs the events that led to the disappearance of the crew.

Player decisions and discovered information influence the progression of the story and its possible endings.

## Key Features

* **Exploration:** Explore an abandoned spacecraft and discover its hidden areas.
* **Investigation System:** Collect evidence, analyze information, and reconstruct past events.
* **Environmental Puzzles:** Solve interactive puzzles involving terminals, security systems, and various ship mechanisms.
* **Interactive Objects:** Interact with computers, logs, documents, and other environmental elements.
* **Narrative Choices:** Make decisions that influence story progression and possible endings.
* **Atmospheric Storytelling:** Discover the story through environmental details, documents, and interactions.

## Gameplay

The core gameplay revolves around:

* First-person exploration.
* Object interaction.
* Investigation and evidence collection.
* Environmental puzzle solving.
* Dialogue and interaction with the ship's AI.
* Narrative progression and branching decisions.
